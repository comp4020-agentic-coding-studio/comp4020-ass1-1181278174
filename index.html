An interactive explainer

No ant knows the map. The road appears anyway — and heals itself when you break it.

Ant colonies find their way with no leader and no map: each ant leaves a little scent where it walks, and follows the stronger scent it meets.

Repeated by hundreds of ants, and slowly forgotten by the ground, that one rule is enough for a road to appear — and it is the idea behind a family of computer algorithms called ant colony optimisation, used for routing and scheduling.

Below is a colony of four hundred simulated ants that follow only that rule. Press Run, then draw a wall across their road and watch what happens.
