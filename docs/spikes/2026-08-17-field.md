# Field spike — 2026-08-17

Decision 16 moved the visitor's fixture to a 60x40 field. **Exploratory only**;
no threshold is derived here. The question was whether Model 1 / mode 1b behaves
on the field as it does on the double bridge.

**It does not.** The geometry is right and the engine runs, but no road forms —
at any field size tested. Numbers below; the finding is at the bottom.

## The fixture

| | |
|---|---|
| grid | 60 x 40, 4-neighbour |
| nodes / edges | 2141 / 4078 |
| blocked cells | 260 (wall of 37, five obstacle blocks) |
| gap | cell `30,11`, sealed by two `shortcut` edges |
| BFS round the wall | **105 moves** |
| BFS through the gap | **53 moves** |
| ratio | **1.981** — the bridge's 2.0, as intended |
| params | h = 2, k = 20, floor = 0 (same as the bridge), 400 ants |

## 1. No road forms

At step 6000 with the gap shut, **3 trips had completed** and the reading never
left "no reading yet" (it needs 65). The ASCII map is uniform noise, not a road:
pheromone is spread across the whole field rather than concentrated anywhere.

## 2-4. The three rates, all meaningless

The reading is trip length / BFS. On the bridge it settles near 1.07x. Here:

| rate | reading after the gap opens | what it means |
|---|---|---|
| rho = 0 | 53.6x rising to **83.8x** over 12000 steps | a 53-move route being walked in ~4400 moves |
| rho = 0.12 | same order | — |
| rho = 0.25 | same order | — |

Nothing can be said about lock-in, switching or instability, because the colony
never finds a route to lock on to in the first place.

## Why — the home field has a gradient, but a useless one

Mean `home` pheromone by BFS distance from the nest (rho = 0.12, 6000 steps, 400
ants):

| distance from nest | mean home tau |
|---|---|
| 0-9 | 1.05 |
| 20-29 | 0.82 |
| 50-59 | 0.56 |
| 80-89 | 0.16 |
| 100-109 | 0.08 |

The gradient is real in aggregate and points the right way. But **between two
adjacent edges it is about 2%**, against a choice weight of `(k + tau)^h`. A
carrier is doing a near-random walk. 114 of 400 ants were holding food at step
6000 and could not get home.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Shrinking the field makes trips *happen*. It does not make a road. Even at 14x10
— 134 nodes, a 21-move route — the colony averages **342 moves per trip**. The
ratio never trends toward 1 at any size.

## The finding

**Mode 1b's feedback is inverted on a 2D field.** With a fixed deposit per step
and no retrace, an ant that wanders 300 steps lays 300 units of pheromone, and an
ant that goes straight home in 21 lays 21. Longer, more diffuse journeys deposit
*more* total pheromone, so nothing concentrates and positive feedback has nothing
to bootstrap from.

The double bridge hid this. With two corridors and no room to wander, every
deposit necessarily lands on one of the two routes — the topology did the
concentrating that the deposit rule does not do. All four behaviours still hold
there; this is not evidence against 1b on the bridge.

The mechanism that makes Ant System work on a general graph is the one Decision
1(b) parked: deposit **Q/L on the completed route**, after the trip, so a short
route gets more pheromone per edge than a long one. That flag was to be "enabled
only on spike evidence". This is that evidence.

**No threshold, parameter or decision was changed to produce any number above.**
